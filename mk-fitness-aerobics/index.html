Joining par fitness assessment

Pehle din hi weight, measurements aur fitness level note hota hai. Yahi baseline aage ke har progress check ka reference banti hai — taaki 'kuch farak nahi pada' wali feeling kabhi aaye hi nahi, kyunki numbers sach bolte hain.

🥗

Khana jo follow ho sake

Diet ka matlab upar se thopa gaya chart nahi. Ghar mein bane khane ko hi thoda smart tareeke se arrange kiya jata hai — dal, sabzi, roti, dahi jaise roz ke khane ke saath protein aur portion ki samajh. Jo plan follow na ho, wo plan hi nahi hota.

Batch wali energy

Subah-shaam ke group batches aur aerobics sessions mein akele train karne ki boreeyat khatm ho jati hai. Saath walo ki regularity dekh kar khud bhi chhootne ka mann nahi karta — members yahi sabse bada fayda batate hain.

Galat form se mili chot mahino peeche dhakel deti hai. Isiliye floor trainers naye members ki form par khaas nazar rakhte hain, aur warm-up/mobility ko optional nahi, routine ka hissa mana jata hai.
