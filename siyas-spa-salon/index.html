Making the most of your spa & salon visit

A detailed, practical guide from the team — the questions customers ask most, answered honestly before you book.

Arrive ten minutes before your slot at Siyas so the consultation does not eat into your service time. For hair services, tell your stylist your routine honestly — how often you wash, heat-style and colour — because the right cut and treatment depend on the life your hair actually lives, not the one you plan to start. If you are considering keratin or smoothening, ask for a strand test and a clear maintenance list before committing; the first wash timing and sulphate-free care decide how long the result lasts.

For spa therapies, mention any injury, pregnancy, blood-pressure issue or skin sensitivity at booking, not at the table, so the therapist can adapt pressure and oils. Bridal bookings work backwards from the wedding date: makeup trial first, then pre-bridal skin sessions spaced weekly, with the final facial at least four to five days before the function — never the day before. Party makeup slots on weekends fill by Friday, so WhatsApp booking is genuinely the difference between a calm chair and a queue.

Carry a photo of the look you want, keep jewellery minimal for facial and spa services, and confirm the final bill before the service begins. Good salons welcome all of these questions — they are how a 4.7-star rating is built.

Why a direct website beats a directory listing

Everything in one place

A directory listing shows a name, a rating and a call button. It cannot explain which service fits your problem, what it costs, how many sittings it needs, or what to do before and after. This page does all of that in one scroll — so by the time you message, you already know what you are asking for, and the conversation starts at confirmation instead of basic questions.

Faster, clearer booking

Directory calls route through masked numbers and can be missed during busy hours. A WhatsApp enquiry from this page carries your chosen service, quantity and preferred date in writing. Nothing gets lost, timings are confirmed in the same thread, and both sides have the same record of what was agreed.

Honest expectations

Good results in skin care, celebrations, grooming and fitness all come from realistic plans — number of sessions, timelines, aftercare and maintenance. The guides and FAQs on this page are written to set those expectations before money changes hands, which is exactly why highly rated businesses benefit most from having a website of their own.

Found on Google, not buried

When someone searches for the service plus the neighbourhood, a business with its own page can be found directly — with photos, prices and a booking button — instead of appearing as one row among fifty competitors on a directory page that also promotes rivals right beside it.
