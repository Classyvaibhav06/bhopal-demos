Dental Implants

A permanent, fixed replacement for a missing tooth — a titanium root placed in the jaw, topped with a natural-looking ceramic crown. No slipping like a denture, no grinding down of neighbouring teeth like a bridge.

  • Assessment of bone & bite before planning
  • Implant placement, healing check, then crown fitting
  • Single tooth, multiple teeth or full-arch options

Sittings: 2–3 visits over a few weeks (placement → healing → crown) · Aftercare: soft diet for the first days, regular brushing & flossing, review visits as advised

Starting at ₹25,000* per implant

Root Canal Treatment (RCT)

Saves a tooth that is deeply decayed or infected — the infected pulp is removed, the canal is cleaned and sealed, and the tooth is usually protected with a crown. The aim: relieve pain and keep your natural tooth.

  • Painless technique with proper anaesthesia
  • Rotary RCT for precise, faster cleaning
  • Ceramic crown option to protect the tooth after

Sittings: often 1–2 (single-visit possible in many cases) · Aftercare: avoid hard food on the tooth until crowned; mild sensitivity for a few days is normal

Starting at ₹4,500* per tooth
🦷

Braces & Clear Aligners

Straighter teeth for teens and adults — traditional braces or near-invisible clear aligners that you remove while eating. The right option depends on your bite and lifestyle; it is decided after a consultation.

  • Periodic reviews to track progress

Sittings: consultation first, then fitting + review visits over the treatment period · Aftercare: aligner wear as advised (typically most of the day), retainer after completion

Consultation from ₹500*

How much does a dental implant cost at Vita Dental?

Indicative demo price on this page starts at ₹25,000* per implant. Your final cost depends on the examination and imaging — bone condition, crown type, and whether any preparatory treatment is needed. You receive the full written plan and cost before deciding.

Which price on this page is confirmed?

Teeth whitening at ₹3,500 is as listed in the clinic's Google services. All other prices marked * are indicative demo prices to help you plan — the clinic confirms your exact cost after a clinical examination, before treatment begins.

Is root canal treatment painful?

With proper local anaesthesia, an RCT should not hurt during the procedure — relieving your existing pain is the point of the treatment. Mild sensitivity for a few days afterwards is normal, and most cases finish in 1–2 sittings.

How long does an implant take from start to finish?

Typically 2–3 visits spread over a few weeks: placement first, a healing period while the implant bonds with the bone, then the crown fitting. Your exact timeline is mapped out in your written plan at the assessment visit.

Braces or clear aligners — which is right for me?

It depends on your bite, the movement needed, and how discreet you need treatment to be. Aligners are removable and near-invisible; braces handle some movements more predictably. A consultation (from ₹500*) decides this properly — book one below.
