How Physiotherapy Works

Not just machines — a method

Physiotherapy treats the cause of pain, not only the symptom. At Activecare, treatment combines hands-on therapy, guided exercise and education — in that order of importance.

1. Hands-on therapy

Manual mobilisation and soft-tissue work ease stiffness and pain so the joint or muscle can move the way it should. This opens the door — it is not the whole treatment.

2. Guided exercise

Targeted strengthening and mobility exercises rebuild what pain and rest have weakened. This is what makes recovery last after your sessions end.

3. Education & habits

Posture, lifting technique, workstation setup and a simple home routine — small daily changes that stop the same pain from coming back.

An honest note

Physiotherapy is progressive — most conditions improve over a course of sessions, not in a single visit. If your assessment suggests you need medical or surgical care first, you will be told so plainly and guided accordingly.

Common Questions

Before you book

Straight answers to what patients ask most. Anything else — ask on WhatsApp.

Do I need a doctor's prescription or reports to start physiotherapy?

No — you can book an assessment directly. If you have X-rays, MRIs or a doctor's notes, bring them along; they help Dr. Priyanka Gupta plan faster. For post-surgery rehab, your surgeon's protocol is followed alongside.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your condition and how long it has been present. Indicatively: posture problems often improve in 6–10 sessions, back/neck pain in 8–12, and post-surgery rehab can run 12–24. Your realistic timeline is confirmed after the first assessment — and reviewed as you progress.

What does a session cost?

Indicative demo prices on this page start around ₹500–₹600 per session depending on the condition. Your exact charges are confirmed by the clinic after assessment, before treatment begins — no surprises mid-plan.

Will the treatment hurt?

Physiotherapy should not be painful. Some exercises can feel like effort or mild discomfort as stiff, weak areas start working again — that is normal and temporary. Anything sharp or worsening is a signal to stop and tell your therapist, and the plan is adjusted.

What happens in the first visit?

A detailed assessment: your history, posture, movement, strength and pain triggers are checked. You will leave knowing what the likely cause is, what your phase-wise plan looks like, how many sessions it may take, and your home routine for day one.

Can I do my exercises at home instead of visiting?

Home exercises are a part of every plan — but they work best alongside supervised sessions, where technique is corrected and the plan is progressed. Skipping supervision is the most common reason home-only attempts stall.
