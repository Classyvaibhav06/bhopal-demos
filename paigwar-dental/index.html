FAQ

Questions patients ask us

How much does a consultation cost?
Consultation is ₹300, as listed on Practo. It includes a full oral examination; if an X-ray is needed, the doctor will tell you why and what it shows before planning anything.
Can a root canal really be finished in one visit?
In many cases, yes — where the infection is limited and the tooth's canals are straightforward, a single-visit RCT is possible. If your tooth needs the infection to settle first, the doctor will explain why two sittings are safer for you.
Will I know the full cost before treatment starts?
Yes. After examination and X-ray, you receive the treatment plan with an indicative cost for each option. The prices on this website are indicative/demo starting prices; your written estimate at the clinic is what you go by.
Do you treat children?
Yes — kids' dentistry is a regular part of the clinic, from first check-ups and fluoride applications to fillings for milk teeth. A kids' visit starts at ₹400* (indicative/demo).
What are the clinic hours?
Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM to 9:30 PM, and Sunday, 9:30 AM to 2:00 PM — as per the clinic's Places listing. For a holiday or an emergency, a quick call or WhatsApp message is the fastest way to confirm.
I'm nervous about pain. How is it handled?
Local anaesthesia is given time to work fully before treatment begins, and the doctor checks in during the procedure. Post-treatment medicines and aftercare are explained in writing so you know exactly what to expect.
How do I book an appointment?
Use the booking form on this page, call +91 99819 85255, or message the clinic on WhatsApp — whichever is easiest. Evening slots fill fastest, so booking a day ahead helps.
