FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I book an appointment?
Pick your service in the booking form below (or tap any "Book" link), and it opens WhatsApp with your details pre-filled. The salon confirms your slot and final price on WhatsApp itself. You can also simply call +91 93436 45203.
Are the prices on this page final?
Prices shown are indicative starting prices for this demo website. Your exact price depends on hair length, product choice and the look you want — it is always confirmed on WhatsApp before your appointment, so you never pay more than agreed.
Do you travel for bridal makeup?
Yes. The bridal team travels to hotels, marriage gardens and homes in Bhopal for bridal bookings, carrying the complete makeup and hairstyling kit. Share your venue in the booking form and the team will plan the timing with you.
What's the difference between HD and airbrush bridal makeup?
HD makeup is applied with traditional professional products and gives a natural, skin-like finish that photographs beautifully. Airbrush makeup is sprayed on in a fine mist — it is lighter, water-resistant and typically lasts longer through a long wedding day. The team will recommend based on your skin type and function schedule.
Is this salon for men too?
Absolutely — it's in the name. Beard styling, men's haircuts, cleanup facials and grooming packages are a big part of the salon, alongside women's hair, brows, makeup and skin services.
How early should I book for wedding season?
Bridal dates in peak shaadi season often book out weeks in advance. Send your function date on WhatsApp as soon as it is fixed — checking availability is free and needs no advance payment.
What are your opening hours?
The salon is open all 7 days, 10:30 AM – 8:30 PM. Bridal and early-morning function bookings are taken by appointment outside these hours as well — just ask on WhatsApp.
Where exactly is the salon?
