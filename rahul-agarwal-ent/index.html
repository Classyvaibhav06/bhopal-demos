Symptom Guide

What's troubling you? Pehle samjhiye.

Apni takleef chunein — common symptoms, kab doctor ko dikhana zaroori hai, aur ilaaj aam taur par kis disha mein hota hai. Yeh general jaankari hai, diagnosis nahi — sahi kaaran examination se hi pata chalta hai.

⚠️ Agar saans lene mein takleef, tez bukhaar ke saath gale/kaan mein soojan, achanak sunai band hona, ya khoon aana ho — to intezaar na karein, turant doctor ya emergency care se sampark karein.

Treatments & Procedures

What happens at the clinic — explained simply

Har treatment mein kya hota hai, pehle hi jaan lijiye. Procedure ki fees examination ke baad confirm hoti hai — consultation fee ₹500 hai, jaisa Justdial par listed hai. (Demo website: koi aur price online listed nahi hai.)

🔬

Ear Examination & Wax Cleaning

Kaan ko microscope/otoscope se dekha jata hai. Jama hua wax surakshit tareeke se nikala jata hai — ghar par ear-bud ya pin ka istemal dard aur infection badha sakta hai, isliye khud saaf karne ki koshish na karein.

OPD procedure · Fee confirmed at clinic

👃

Nasal Endoscopy Evaluation

Ek patli camera-wali tube se naak aur sinus ke raste ko andar se dekha jata hai — band naak, baar-baar sinus ya naak ki haddi tedhi (DNS) jaise kaaran saaf nazar aate hain, aur ilaaj ki disha tai hoti hai.

OPD evaluation · Fee confirmed at clinic

🌡️

Sinusitis Treatment

Sinus ka ilaaj aksar dawa, steam aur nasal spray se shuru hota hai. Lamba chalne wale ya baar-baar ke cases mein CT scan aur zaroorat padne par endoscopic sinus surgery (FESS) par vichaar hota hai — nirnay examination ke baad hi liya jata hai.

Medicine first · Surgery only if needed

🗣️

Tonsil & Adenoid Care

Baar-baar tonsil infection, kharrate ya neend mein saans rukna — pehle dawa aur observation. Jab infection saal mein kai baar ho ya saans/neend par asar pade, tab tonsillectomy ki salah di ja sakti hai, woh bhi poori jaanch ke baad.

For kids & adults · Advice after examination

🎧

Hearing Test Guidance

Sunai kam dena ya kaan mein seeti jaisi awaaz (tinnitus) ho to hearing test (audiometry) se samasya ki gehrai pata chalti hai. Kaaran ke hisaab se dawa, safai ya hearing aid ki disha tai hoti hai — jaldi jaanch, behtar result.

Audiometry guidance · Fee confirmed at clinic

🌀

Vertigo & Balance Assessment

Ghoomne jaisa chakkar (vertigo) aksar kaan ke balance system se juda hota hai. Examination se BPPV, kaan ka infection ya anya kaaran alag kiye jate hain, aur usi ke hisaab se dawa ya special exercises diye jate hain.

Cause-based treatment · OPD assessment

🤧

Allergy Management

Baar-baar chheenk, naak behna aur aankhon mein khujli — pehle trigger pehchana jata hai (dhool, pollen, thand), phir spray, dawa aur bachav ka lamba plan banta hai taaki baar-baar ki takleef kam ho.

Long-term plan · Trigger-based care

🎤

Voice & Throat Care

Lambi awaaz baithna, gale mein kuch atka mehsoos hona ya baar-baar kharaash — gala aur vocal cords ki jaanch se kaaran pata chalta hai. Teachers, singers aur zyada bolne walon ke liye voice-care salah bhi di jati hai.

OPD evaluation · Fee confirmed at clinic

😴

Snoring Evaluation

Tez kharrate aur neend mein saans ka rukna (sleep apnoea) sirf shor nahi hai — din ki thakaan aur sehat par asar pad sakta hai. Naak-gale ki jaanch se rukawat ki jagah pata chalti hai aur ilaaj ki disha tai hoti hai.

Sleep & breathing check · Advice after examination
