Eye-health basics

Small habits that protect your sight

General awareness information for every age group. If anything here sounds familiar, an eye check-up is a sensible next step.

🖥️

The 20-20-20 rule

Long screen hours tire the eyes and cause dryness and headaches. Every 20 minutes, look at something about 20 feet away for 20 seconds — and blink fully and often.

🩸

Diabetes can silently damage the retina

High blood sugar can harm the tiny blood vessels at the back of the eye long before vision changes. People with diabetes should get a dilated retina check at least once a year.

🎂

After 40, check every year

Cataract, glaucoma and near-vision changes (presbyopia) all become common after 40 — and glaucoma often has no early symptoms. A yearly check catches them early.

🧒

Watch your child's eyes

Squinting, sitting very close to the TV, frequent eye rubbing or falling grades can signal a vision problem. Early treatment of lazy eye (amblyopia) works best in childhood.

🕶️

Protect against sun, dust & sparks

Use sunglasses outdoors and proper safety glasses for grinding, welding or farm work. Many serious eye injuries happen during everyday tasks at home and work.

💧

Never self-medicate eye drops

Steroid drops used without a doctor's advice can raise eye pressure and worsen infections. If redness or pain lasts more than a day or two, get examined instead of guessing.
