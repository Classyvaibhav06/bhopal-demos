How to order a custom cake that arrives right

A detailed, practical guide from the team — the questions customers ask most, answered honestly before you book.

A custom cake goes wrong in predictable ways, and all of them are avoidable in the WhatsApp order itself. Always state five things together: the date AND time you need the cake, the flavour, the weight or number of servings, whether it must be eggless, and the exact name or message to write on top — spelling included. For theme cakes, send one clear reference photo rather than five different ones, and say which element matters most: the character, the colour theme, or the shape.

Tier cakes need transport planning, so share whether you will pick up or need delivery, and the delivery area in Indore, in the first message itself. Fondant cakes should be ordered 24–48 hours ahead; fresh-cream cakes are more flexible but still taste best baked the same day. If the cake is for a child, mention nut allergies and colour preferences clearly.

For midnight surprises, confirm the slot a day in advance and keep a phone reachable at delivery time. Store cream cakes refrigerated and take them out 15–20 minutes before cutting so the sponge softens. Cookies and dry cakes keep well in an airtight box for several days and are the safest choice for outstation gifting.

When in doubt, order half a kilo more than your headcount — running short at a celebration is the one problem no bakery can fix afterwards.
