Welcome to Gurukripa

A family table at 6 Number Stop

Hotel New Gurukripa is Shivaji Nagar's pure-veg family restaurant on Link Road 1 — the stop where neighbourhood families, office groups and travellers sit down for a proper meal. One kitchen serves four cuisines, so everyone at the table gets what they actually want: a full North Indian thali, a crisp masala dosa, wok-tossed noodles, or a quick pav bhaji.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is Hotel New Gurukripa fully vegetarian?

Yes — it is a 100% pure veg family restaurant. Every dish across North Indian, South Indian, Chinese and fast food is vegetarian.

What are the opening hours?

We are open all 7 days, 10:30 AM to 11:30 PM.

How do I order without a food delivery app?

Build your order in the menu section above, add a pickup/delivery note, and tap "Order on WhatsApp". Your order reaches the kitchen directly — no app commission and no aggregator markup. Prices shown are indicative and confirmed on WhatsApp.

Can I book a table for a family function or kitty party?

Yes. Use the booking form above with your date, time and guest count, and the team will confirm your table on WhatsApp. For groups of 12 or more, booking ahead helps us keep seating and service ready.

Is takeaway available?

Yes — takeaway is packed fresh and hygienically. Order ahead on WhatsApp and it will be ready when you arrive at 6 Number Stop.

Where exactly is the restaurant?

6 Number Stop, Link Road 1, near Shree Krishna Dairy, Shivaji Nagar, Bhopal. Tap "Get directions" in the Visit section below.

Which dishes is Gurukripa known for?

The Special Thali is the signature, alongside paneer butter masala, masala dosa, chole bhature and pav bhaji — spanning all four cuisines on the menu.
