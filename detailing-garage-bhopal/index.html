    Before you decide

    Why protect your paint at all?

    Your car's paint is thinner than a human hair is wide, and Bhopal is not gentle with it: summer sun fades it, hard water spots it, highway drives chip it, and every rough wash swirls it. Wax lasts weeks. Real protection lasts years — but the two kinds work very differently.

    🛡️ Paint Protection Film (PPF)

    Physical armour · typically lasts 5–7 years

    Think of it as a transparent shield stuck over the paint. Stones, keys, shopping carts and minor brushes hit the film — not your car. Light scratches in the film disappear with heat ("self-healing").

    • Best against: stone chips, scratches, parking scrapes
    • Can be applied full-body or only to high-impact zones
    • Highest protection — and the highest price
    • Choose it for: new cars, dark colours, highway driving

    💎 Ceramic Coating

    Chemical gloss shield · typically lasts 2–5 years

    A nano-ceramic layer bonded to the clear coat. It won't stop a stone chip, but it makes the paint dramatically glossier, repels water and dust, blocks UV fading, and makes washing effortless.

    • Best against: UV fade, water spots, dust sticking, dullness
    • Deep "wet look" gloss that wax can't match
    • Far more affordable than PPF
    • Choose it for: gloss lovers, easier maintenance, daily drivers

    Still confused? Many owners do both — PPF on the bumper, bonnet and mirrors (the impact zones) plus ceramic everywhere for the gloss. Build that combo in the estimator above and we'll price it exactly on WhatsApp.

    Good questions

    Before you book

    How long does each job take?

    Exterior washes take 2–3 hours, complete detailing about a full day, paint correction 1–2 days depending on stages, and ceramic coating or PPF 2–3 days including curing time. We're open 9:30 AM – 9:00 PM, all 7 days, so drop-offs and pickups are flexible.

    PPF or ceramic coating — which one should I choose?

    If your fear is scratches, stone chips and parking damage, that's PPF — it's physical armour. If your goal is gloss, easier washing and UV protection at a friendlier price, that's ceramic. Many owners combine both: PPF on impact zones, ceramic on the rest. The education section above explains it in detail.

    Are the prices on this website final?

    They're honest indicative ranges. Your exact quote depends on your car's size and its paint condition, which is why the estimator asks for both. Send the estimate to us on WhatsApp and we'll confirm the final price before you visit — the price we confirm is the price you pay.

    Do I need an appointment, or can I just come?

    Walk-ins are welcome for washes and inspections. For detailing, correction, ceramic and PPF, booking a slot (form below or WhatsApp) guarantees we keep a bay and a full day free for your car.

    Will detailing remove all my scratches?

    Paint correction removes swirls, light scratches and oxidation that sit in the clear coat. Deep scratches that have cut through to the primer can't be polished out — those need paint work. At the free paint check, we'll show you under the lights exactly what will and won't come out, before you spend anything.

    How do I maintain the finish after detailing or coating?

    Simple rules: wash with the two-bucket method or a gentle foam wash, avoid roadside rough-cloth washes, dry with microfiber, and never wipe a dusty car dry. At delivery we explain the full routine in two minutes — and ceramic/PPF cars get priority slots for maintenance washes.
