Our Story

A Family Table in the Heart of Old Bhopal

Zam Zam is not just a fast-food counter — it is a piece of Old Bhopal. Run by the family behind the counter, in the lanes opposite one of India's grandest mosques, it has grown into a culinary destination for the whole of Madhya Pradesh.

Food travellers arriving by rail and road make a beeline here for a taste of Bhopal's Mughlai heritage — recipes built on slow marinades, charcoal smoke and generous, share-sized platters.

  • Family-run, in the historic Peer Gate neighbourhood
  • Charcoal-grilled kebabs & tikkas, made through the day
  • Mandi platters built for families and groups
  • A must-stop on every Old Bhopal food walk
