Quick answers

Are the prices on the cards final?

Prices are indicative, to help you shortlist. Tap "Inquire with SKU" and we'll confirm today's price for that exact piece on WhatsApp — along with any running offer.

Can I get a carpet or curtain in a different size?

Many pieces come in multiple sizes, and curtains can often be matched to your window or door length. Send the SKU with the size you need and we'll tell you what's possible.

Do you deliver in Bhopal?

We serve MP Nagar, Arera Colony, Kolar and the wider Bhopal area. WhatsApp us the SKU and your locality, and we'll confirm delivery options for your address before you order.
