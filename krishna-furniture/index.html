Gehrai se

Furniture kharidne se pehle ye jaano

Furniture roz kharida nahi jata — ek galat faisla saalon tak roz dikhta hai. 25 saalon ke anubhav se Krishna Furniture har grahak ko wahi salah deta hai jo apne ghar ke liye leta.

🪵

Lakdi ki saaf jaankari

Sheesham, engineered wood ya mixed material — jo hai, wahi bataya jata hai. Frame ki lakdi aur bahari finish mein farak hota hai, aur ye farak tumhe kharidne se pehle samjhaya jata hai, baad mein chhupaya nahi jata.

📐

Size pehle napo, phir chuno

Sabse aam galti — showroom mein chhota laga sofa ghar ki deewar kha jata hai. Kamre ki measurement ke hisaab se size suggest hota hai, aur zarurat padne par pehle ghar ka layout discuss karke hi design final hota hai.

🧰

Joint aur finishing ki parakh

Paanch saal baad furniture wahi bolta hai jo uske joints bolte hain. Yahan har piece ki joinery, drawer channel aur polish ki finishing delivery se pehle check hoti hai — kyunki dukaan ka naam har piece ke saath judta hai.

💰

Budget mein best combination

Fixed budget mein poora ghar sajana ek kala hai — kahan sheesham lena faydemand hai aur kahan engineered wood samajhdari hai, ye salah bina apna fayda dekhe di jati hai. Package rates isiliye alag-alag lene se hamesha behtar padte hain.
