The Krishnam Promise

Why families trust us with their gold

Rated 4.9★ across 693 Justdial ratings — here's what that trust is built on, purchase after purchase.

🏵️

HUID hallmark purity

Every gold piece carries its BIS HUID hallmark — the unique ID stamped on the jewellery that you can verify yourself. The purity on the tag is the purity in the metal, always.

🧾

Transparent making charges

Rate, weight, making charges and GST are broken up clearly on the bill before you pay — no bundled "discount" maths, no last-minute additions at the counter.

🔁

Honest exchange value

Old gold is tested for purity in front of you and valued at the day's rate. Exchange your old pieces towards a new design, or upgrade within the family — the valuation is explained, not assumed.

✍️

Craftsmanship & custom orders

Seen a design on Instagram or in a family album? Share the photo — our karigars recreate and customise it in hallmarked gold, with the design and estimate confirmed before work begins.
