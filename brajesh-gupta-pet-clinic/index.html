Vaccination Planner

When is your pet's next vaccine due?

Most pet parents miss boosters simply because nobody told them the dates. Here's the usual schedule vets follow — Dr. Gupta confirms your pet's exact plan at the visit.

6–8 weeks

First core vaccines begin

The puppy series starts — protection against distemper, parvovirus and other early-life diseases. First deworming rounds usually begin around this age too.

10–12 weeks

Second round

Booster doses build on the first round. A good time for a full health check, weight review and diet guidance as your puppy grows fast.

14–16 weeks

Rabies vaccination

The anti-rabies dose completes the puppy series — legally important and essential for every dog in India.

Every year

Annual boosters + deworming

Boosters once a year, deworming roughly every 3 months for adult dogs (general guidance). Tick & flea prevention is reviewed at the same visit.

7+ years

Senior care

Twice-a-year check-ups, dental and joint review, and screening tests as advised — older dogs age quietly, so regular visits catch problems early.

6–8 weeks

First kitten vaccines begin

The kitten series starts with core protection, alongside the first deworming doses and a litter, diet & growth check.

10–12 weeks

Second round

Booster doses complete the core series. Coat, teeth and weight are checked as your kitten moves to solid food.

14–16 weeks

Rabies vaccination

The anti-rabies dose — needed for outdoor cats and recommended for indoor cats too.

Every year

Annual boosters + deworming

Yearly boosters, deworming every 3–6 months (general guidance), and a dental, weight & coat check — plus an indoor/outdoor risk review.

7+ years

Senior care

Check-ups every 6 months with kidney, thyroid and dental screening as advised — cats hide illness well, so routine visits matter.

    From the Clinic

    Small habits that keep pets healthy

    General guidance every pet parent can follow at home — personalised advice for your pet comes at the visit.

    🌧️

    Monsoon = tick season

    Bhopal's rains bring ticks and fleas out in force. Check ears, neck and between the toes after walks, and keep prevention doses on schedule — ask at the clinic which option suits your pet.

    ☀️

    Beat the Bhopal summer

    Walk dogs early morning or after sunset, keep fresh water down all day, and never leave a pet in a parked car. Birds and rabbits need shade and ventilation — heat hits them faster.

    🪥

    Teeth tell the truth

    Bad breath is often the first sign of dental trouble. Lift the lip once a month — yellow tartar, red gums or a loose tooth means it's time for a dental check.

    🗓️

    Don't let deworming slip

    Worms quietly steal nutrition, especially from puppies and kittens. Note the next due date on your phone calendar the day it's given — and ask the clinic to confirm the schedule for your pet.
