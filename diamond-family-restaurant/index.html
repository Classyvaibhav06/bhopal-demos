Good to know

Quick answers before you plan your halt or booking.

What are the restaurant's timings?

Public listings show Diamond Family Restaurant & Dhaba open 24 hours, all days. Timings can change on festivals or for maintenance — a quick WhatsApp message confirms before a late-night halt.

Where exactly is the restaurant?

On Narsinghgarh Road, near Axis Bank, at Parwaliya Sadak / Chandukhedi, Bhopal 462030 — right on the highway stretch, so travellers don't need to enter city traffic for a meal break.

Can I order food before I arrive?

Yes. Build your order in the menu above and send it on WhatsApp, or simply message your order to +91 96308 57234. Mention your arrival time and the kitchen can keep it ready.

Do you take table bookings for families?

Yes — use the Book a Table form above. It is especially worth booking ahead for weekend dinners and groups of 8 or more.

Can you handle a big group or a wedding-party halt?

Groups are a regular part of highway business here. Use the Group Booking form with your date, time and guest count (15–500) and the restaurant will confirm seating and per-plate pricing on WhatsApp.

Is there both veg and non-veg food?

Yes — the menu spans veg mains and thalis, dhaba-style chicken and egg dishes, Chinese, breads, rice and desserts. Every dish is marked Veg / Non-Veg in the menu above.

Are the prices on this website final?

No — this is a demo website and all prices are indicative/demo. Your final bill or group quote is always confirmed by the restaurant on WhatsApp or at the counter.
