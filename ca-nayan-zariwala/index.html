GST & Compliance Due-Date Calendar

The recurring dates our clients plan around. These are standard statutory due dates for general reference — exact applicability varies by registration type, turnover and state notifications.

Every month

  • 7thTDS / TCS depositDeposit tax deducted/collected for the previous month.
  • 11thGSTR-1Outward supplies statement (monthly filers).
  • 13thGSTR-1 (quarterly, QRMP)Quarterly GSTR-1 / Invoice Furnishing Facility window.
  • 20thGSTR-3BSummary return & tax payment (monthly filers).
  • 22nd / 24thGSTR-3B (quarterly, QRMP)Staggered by state for quarterly filers.
  • 25thPMT-06 challanMonthly tax payment for QRMP quarterly filers.
  • 30thTDS returns & payroll compliancesQuarterly TDS statements fall due in the month after each quarter.

Key annual dates

  • 15 Jun / Sep / Dec / MarAdvance tax instalmentsPayable in four instalments — 15% / 45% / 75% / 100% cumulative.
  • 31 JulIncome-tax return — individualsITR due date for non-audit cases (as extended by CBDT notifications, if any).
  • 31 OctIncome-tax return — audit casesCompanies and assessees covered by tax audit.
  • 30 SepTax audit reportFurnishing of audit report u/s 44AB ahead of the return due date.
  • AGM +30 / +60 daysROC annual filingsAOC-4 (financial statements) within 30 days and MGT-7 (annual return) within 60 days of the AGM.
  • 31 DecGSTR-9 / 9CGST annual return and reconciliation statement for the financial year.
Educational reference only. Due dates are frequently extended by official notifications. The firm maintains a live compliance calendar for every client — book a consultation to get yours set up.
