Yes — event packages can include in-house catering with custom veg and non-veg menus. Menu and per-plate details are confirmed on WhatsApp.

Standard hotel timings apply; early check-in and late checkout can be added in the estimator as an add-on, subject to availability.

Yes, business stays can be billed with GST invoice details — mention it in your WhatsApp enquiry.
