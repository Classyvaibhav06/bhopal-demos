Before your first visit

A detailed, practical guide from the team — the questions customers ask most, answered honestly before you book.

A consultation at Skin Aura Clinic works best when you arrive with a little preparation. Note down when the concern started, what products or home remedies you have already tried, any medicines you take regularly, and whether the problem changes with season, stress or diet. For hair fall, recent illness, thyroid or hormonal history matters, so mention it without being asked.

Bring photos of how a scar, pigmentation patch or tattoo looked earlier if you have them — change over time helps the doctor judge depth and response. Avoid starting a new strong cream or scrub in the three days before a peel or laser sitting, and tell the clinic about any recent facial, waxing or threading so the skin is not over-treated. During the consultation, ask three things clearly: what exactly is being treated, how many sittings are realistically needed, and what result is NOT promised.

A good clinic answers all three plainly. After a procedure, follow the written aftercare — sunscreen, moisturiser and avoiding picking or scrubbing matter more than any expensive product. If redness, pain or blistering appears after laser work, message the clinic on WhatsApp the same day instead of waiting for the next sitting.

Keep every prescription and bill; skin treatments are courses, not one-day events, and your record makes the next sitting safer and more effective.
