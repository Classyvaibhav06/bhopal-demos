Surgery Care Guide

Before & after your surgery

The information patients look for online and rarely find — a simple guide for most ENT surgeries at the clinic.

📋 Before surgery

  • Complete the blood tests and scans advised, and carry all reports on the day
  • Tell the doctor about every medicine you take — especially blood thinners, diabetes and BP medicines
  • Fast for the hours advised before anaesthesia (usually no food or water after midnight)
  • Inform the clinic about allergies, asthma, or previous anaesthesia problems
  • Arrange for a family member to accompany you and take you home

🏠 After surgery — recovery basics

  • Take medicines exactly as prescribed and finish the full course
  • Rest for the first few days; avoid heavy lifting and straining
  • Attend every follow-up visit — dressings, pack or splint removal and cleaning happen there
  • Don't blow your nose, poke, or insert anything in the operated ear/nose unless advised
  • Don't skip follow-ups because you "feel fine" — healing inside needs checking
  • Don't wait on warning signs: heavy bleeding, high fever, worsening pain or swelling — call the clinic immediately

These are general guidelines for common ENT procedures. Your written instructions from Dr. Gurjar always come first — follow those over anything on this page.
