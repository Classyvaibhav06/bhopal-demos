Inside the Clinic

Technology behind the results

Medical-grade platforms, chosen for Indian skin tones and operated under a dermatologist's supervision — not a salon floor.

🔆

Fractional Laser Platform

Micro-column laser resurfacing used for acne scars and texture refinement — stimulating collagen renewal in targeted zones while surrounding skin supports faster recovery.

💉

PRP Growth-Factor Therapy

Platelet-rich plasma prepared from your own blood in a sterile, single-use protocol, and delivered to the scalp to support hair regrowth across a planned session series.

✨

Laser Hair Reduction

Targeted laser energy for long-term hair reduction, with settings calibrated to skin type at consultation for comfort and safety.

🧴

Medical Peels & Medi-Facials

Dermatologist-selected chemical peels and clinical facials for pigmentation, dullness and maintenance — prescribed by skin condition, not sold as one-size-fits-all packages.
